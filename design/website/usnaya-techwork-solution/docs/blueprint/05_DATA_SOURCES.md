# Data Sources and Content Governance

Approved sources: owner-supplied company facts/brand assets,
owner-approved service descriptions/policies/contact details, original
assets, and properly licensed third-party assets.

Do not invent services, clients, staff, certifications, policies, or
results. Use neutral wording or say information is unavailable until
verified.

Maintain a source register: content/asset ID, claim, owner/source,
verification date, licence/permission, approved-for-public-display flag,
notes/expiry.

AI knowledge must use approved content only. If information is missing
or contradictory, characters acknowledge uncertainty. Do not send
confidential documents to an AI provider without explicit authorization.

For every asset record creator/source URL/licence/attribution/commercial
permission/modification status. Downloadable does not mean commercially
reusable.
